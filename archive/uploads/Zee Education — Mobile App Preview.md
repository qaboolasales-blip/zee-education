# Zee Education — Mobile App Preview

Updated standalone mobile UI preview based on the newly supplied design.

## Updated UI/UX

- Material 3-inspired tonal surfaces, rounded components and active navigation indicator.
- Dark/light appearance toggle in the app header.
- Improved floating five-item navigation: Home, Attendance, Results, Tools and Profile.
- Coordinated multicolour KPI cards and weekly activity charts on every role dashboard.
- Secure-looking QR/manual onboarding screen design.
- Visible vertical scrollbar is hidden while content remains scrollable.

## Role-based tools

- **Parent:** fees/receipts, timetable, homework, notices, leave, messages, calendar and documents/cards.
- **Student:** timetable, homework, notices, calendar, messages, leave and documents/cards.
- **Teacher:** timetable, attendance, marks, homework, messages, notices, reports and leave.
- **School Admin:** students, admissions, fees, staff, timetable, result review, notices, reports, documents and settings.

This is an interactive frontend prototype. QR scanning, authentication, real school data, message delivery, reports/exports and payment actions are not connected yet.
