# Zee Education — School Dashboard UI

Responsive school workspace prototype with a dark command header, desktop side panel, tablet/mobile hamburger drawer and mobile bottom navigation.

## Designed screens

- Overview
- Students
- Attendance
- Academics / results review
- Fees & manual payment records
- Staff & HR
- Timetable & homework
- Reports, print and templates
- School settings, language, theme and app connection

## UI notes

- Eight school base themes: Brown, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Navy Blue and Purple.
- Controlled multi-colour KPIs/charts and semantic statuses.
- English/Roman Urdu/Urdu RTL option is represented in settings.
- This is an interactive frontend UI prototype. Backend, permissions, QR generation, exports and messaging are not connected.
