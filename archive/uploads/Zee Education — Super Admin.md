# Zee Education — Super Admin UI

Responsive platform-control prototype with a dark command header, desktop side panel, tablet/mobile hamburger drawer and mobile quick navigation.

## Designed screens

- Platform overview
- Schools & dedicated installations
- Plans & add-ons
- Billing & renewals
- Custom / built-in print templates
- Support tickets
- System settings

## UI notes

- Commercial line items are shown separately: setup, first-year license, renewal and hosting/domain.
- Per-school negotiated pricing and one-time custom-design rule are represented in the UI.
- Installation/database states are visual demo data only; no provisioning, email or backend is connected.
